## What I would change

**The offer.**

- Score a minute on submit-OK, not on the commanded number. Change PLAN line 36 to "submit-OK within 10 percent of the target". Keep Mini's accepted non-coinbase count beside it, as COUNT.md line 19 already says, unaveraged.
- Say in WHY.md that HOLD-3 and HOLD-4 are overload steps. Write the expected backlog before the run: about 1,000 per second over the ceiling at HOLD-4, plus families and base load.
- Add one per-target ceiling on time, written before 11:20 UTC, after which the target is logged as not met and the run moves on or stops. A miss is still a result, as TRIES line 7 already says. Without it, item 3 can hold the run open until the lane empties.
- Spread the payment senders across every public node that answers synced at 11:20, one process per node (10-06 README line 47: short chains and one process per node kept orphans near zero). Skip a node that does not answer. Do not stack two processes on one node.

**The fee.**

- The payment fee sits between the two family cohorts. At 150 the flood is above families 1–13 and below families 14–16. In full blocks that ordering decides most of the cohort question in WHY.md line 47 before the run starts. Either put payments at 100, so the cohort test is 2× against 1× inside a 1× flood, or keep 150 and write the prediction into WHY.md: families 1–13 slow at HOLD-3 and HOLD-4, families 14–16 hold.
- Expect a small gap even so. In the 6 Oct saturated hold, 1.5× bought about 2 seconds at the median and the tail did not move (10-06 README line 56).
- Split the fee-reject rule. A reject for "mempool full" is not a reject for the rate. Log the reason string. Stop the sender only on a real rate reject.
- Payment fees cost real tKAS. At about 1,624 grams for a signed one-in one-out (10-06 README line 186) and 150 sompi per gram, each accepted payment costs about 0.0024 tKAS. That is about 7.4 tKAS per second at 3,040 accepted, about 26,700 per hour. This is my arithmetic from those two numbers, not a measurement.

**The arm.**

- Write the family lane size at the 11:50 recount, and the minutes to pool-low at the confirm rate. The confirm ran 1,111.1 hops per family per 900 s ([confirm METHOD](https://github.com/STP-KAS/tn10-storm-2026-11-08-confirm/blob/main/logs/METHOD.md) line 31). Sixteen families at that rate land about 19.8 hops per second, which locks about 71,000 tKAS per hour at about 1 tKAS per hop (TRIES line 37). The lane size is not in this repo. Data missing.
- Give the desk a read for PLAN lines 31–34 during the run, or move those conditions to what the desk can read from its submit path. Mini stays the only chain count either way.
- Stop this machine's six miners before the window, at a fixed time written in the plan, early enough that Mini reads 8 or more merged blocks per second without them before 11:40 UTC. They load public nodes the senders will use and they tie the block rate to this machine's freezes. They should also not be running during Tuesday 18:20–22:15 UTC. That is the operator's call. This round does not stop them.
- Fix the tn-master rows in item 8 before the operator pastes anything.
- The desk node: the reason in TRIES line 23 is not supported by the logs (items 5 and 6). Stopping it is a choice, not a fix. If it stays stopped, write that a later try may restart it as a repair, as TRIES line 41 already allows for the next neuron.
